Frequently asked

The practical stuff, answered upfront.

How big are the classes?+

Capped at 12 people for regular sessions, and up to 20 for Super Saturday.

I'm not that fit — will I keep up?+

Every session is scaled by our coaches, so it works whether you're brand new or years into training.

What happens if it's raining?+

We've got undercover space at both locations, so wet weather doesn't cancel your session.

Can I freeze my membership?+

Yes — freezes of 1 to 8 weeks are available for travel, injury or anything else life throws up.

Is there a minimum commitment?+

Memberships run on a minimum 6-week term, with flexible session use across the week.

Are babies and prams welcome?+

Absolutely — our mums & bubs sessions are built specifically for training with your baby along.
